## Summary

- The app analyses sound from your microphone **on your phone** while you
  track a night.
- **Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.** The app has no account, no
  server, no analytics and no advertising.
- Your sleep data stays on your phone until you delete it or choose to
  export it.

## What the app uses and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone audio while tracking | To detect snoring, breathing and movement sounds and estimate sleep stages | Analysed in memory on the phone, then discarded |
| Short snore clips (optional, on by default) | So you can listen to your snoring | Saved on the phone only; deleted automatically after the period you choose (7–90 days) |
| Sleep results (stages, snore counts, scores, alarm times) | To show reports and trends | Saved on the phone only |
| Tags you add (e.g. "Alcohol") | To compare nights | Saved on the phone only |
| Settings (alarm time, window) | To remember your preferences | Saved on the phone only |

The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any
identifier, and does not use tracking.

## Sharing

We do not receive or share your data. If you use **Export**, the app creates
a file and opens your phone's share sheet; where it goes is your choice.

## Other people's sounds

The microphone may pick up sounds from others in the room. Snore clips could
contain their sounds. You can turn clip saving off in Settings.

## Deleting your data

- Delete one night from its report.
- Delete everything in Settings → Delete all data.
- Uninstalling the app removes all its data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under [13/16].

## Not medical advice

The app gives wellness information only. It is not a medical device and does
not diagnose or treat any condition.
